Roster keeps the ASF's bodies (committees, PMCs and the others), the people in
them, their offices, invitations and account requests. It is one of the services
that replace Whimsy; the plan is in `apache/tooling-migration`.

This page grows with the tool. Each section below describes one role's work;
the ones marked *yours* are the roles you hold.

## Who can see what

The roster holds personal data, so only the Secretary can see it. ASF members
at large have no access.
